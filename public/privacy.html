Privacy Policy

Effective July 28, 2026

Threadline is designed to minimize the personal information it handles. This policy explains what data is processed when you use the service.

Resume and job-description text

Text you submit is transmitted to our application infrastructure and AI provider solely to generate the requested analysis. Threadline does not intentionally save this text to an application database. Infrastructure and service providers may process transient logs subject to their own retention and security practices.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Threadline does not receive or store complete card details. Stripe may collect billing, payment, fraud-prevention, and transaction information under its privacy policy.

Technical data

Hosting and security providers may process IP address, browser, device, request, and diagnostic data required to deliver and protect the service.

AI processing

Submitted text is sent to Anthropic to produce the analysis. Do not submit sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for evaluating your job fit.

Your choices

You may use the free analysis without creating an account. You may stop processing by not submitting documents. For privacy questions or requests, use the support channel published with the service.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service evolves. The effective date above identifies the current version.
